# 🚀 LeadPilot

> AI Copilot for Engineering Leaders

LeadPilot is an AI-powered platform that helps Team Leads and Engineering Managers better understand their teams by combining engineering metrics, qualitative observations, and AI-generated coaching insights.

Instead of focusing on productivity scores or developer rankings, LeadPilot provides context that helps leaders prepare for 1:1 meetings, identify coaching opportunities, and support developer growth.

---

# Why LeadPilot?

Engineering leaders constantly switch between multiple tools:

* GitHub
* Jira
* Slack
* Documentation
* Customer conversations
* Personal notes

Important information is scattered across different systems, making it difficult to understand what is really happening inside a team.

LeadPilot consolidates technical and human signals into a single platform.

---

# Core Principles

LeadPilot is **NOT** a productivity monitoring tool.

LeadPilot is **NOT** an employee surveillance platform.

LeadPilot is an AI assistant that helps engineering leaders:

* Understand context
* Prepare for conversations
* Identify coaching opportunities
* Recognize achievements
* Detect risks early

The AI never replaces human judgment.

## Human Events

Engineering leaders can manually register observations such as:

* Customer praise
* Technical leadership
* Communication improvements
* Critical incidents
* Mentoring moments

---

## AI Insights

Using both engineering metrics and qualitative observations, the AI generates:

* Coaching suggestions
* Risks
* Positive highlights
* Weekly summaries

# Development Philosophy

Every feature must help an engineering leader answer at least one of these questions:

* What changed this week?
* Who needs support?
* Who deserves recognition?
* What should I discuss during my next 1:1?
* What risks should I know about?

If a feature does not improve leadership decisions, it probably should not exist.
